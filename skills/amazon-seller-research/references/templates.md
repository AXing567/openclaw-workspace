## 3. Outreach templates

### Initial outreach

Hi {{name}}, I’m speaking with {{segment}} sellers to understand how they currently handle {{problem-area}}. You seem relevant because {{specific-signal}}. Would you be open to a short chat or a few written answers?

### Warmer variant

Hi {{name}}, I’ve been researching how {{segment}} sellers deal with {{problem-area}}, and your business stood out because {{specific-signal}}. I’m not selling anything in this message — just trying to learn from people doing the work. If you’re open, I’d love to ask a few short questions.

### Follow-up

Just bumping this once in case it got buried — if {{problem-area}} is relevant on your side, I’d still value a quick perspective. If not, no worries.

### Screener

To make sure I’m talking to the right people: are you currently selling on {{marketplace}}, and does your team directly handle {{workflow}}?

## 4. Interview guide template

### Core questions

1. Walk me through how you currently handle {{workflow/problem}}.
2. What is the most annoying part of that process?
3. How often does this problem come up?
4. What have you tried already?
5. What works, and what still breaks?
6. Who on your team feels this pain most directly?
7. When do you decide a problem is worth paying to solve?
8. What would make you distrust a solution in this area?

### Follow-up probes

- Can you give me a recent example?
- What happened next?
- How much time or money did that cost?
- Why do you think that workaround exists?
- If nothing changed, what would the downside be?

### Avoid

- “Would you use this product idea?”
- “Don’t you think X is a big problem?”
- Any question that embeds your proposed solution too early
